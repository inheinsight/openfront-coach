## 1. Are the coach's core numbers still exact? (claim-by-claim audit table)

### Takeaway
Most raw numbers are still exact: troop cap, City +250k, regen formula and its ~42% peak, flat 100 gold/tick, every cost ladder, the 0.6–2 clamp, the 25% retreat malus, the 30 s traitor timer, the 80% win bar and the spawn-phase lengths. The coach goes wrong in how it explains and applies some of them. The attack-loss formula now also has a troop-density term and large-territory discounts. The bot 70% multiplier is described the wrong way round. The nation-betrayal rule is invented. Public FFA has an overtime rule that lowers the win bar. And in public Team lobbies there are no nations at all.

### Inferences
- **Attack ledger.** The coach's 0.6–2 clamp language should be rewritten: below ~50% of the target's home troops you pay the 2× cap, and from ~167% up you pay the 0.6 floor. It should also note that the density term `0.0039 × defenderTroops/defenderTiles` makes small, dense defenders expensive per tile, and that both sides' territory-size sigmoids (midpoint 300k tiles) make giant empires cheaper to attack from and into. A ratio-only verdict will misjudge attacks on dense targets.
- **"Whole army" vs home troops.** The engine compares against the defender's home troops each tick. Attacking a player just after they launch a big attack elsewhere is mechanically cheaper. The coach's "compare your stack with their whole army" overstates the cost of opportunistic attacks.
- **Opening (inference from the engine formula, not tested in game).** Troops inside an attack are removed from `player.troops()` ([AttackExecution.ts:131-138](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/7ee96860c717b1ea36870cdfa6d4bb1e9bccd6de/packages/engine/src/execution/AttackExecution.ts#L131-L138)), and regen is computed on home troops. So a 100% send drops regen to ~10/tick, versus ~350/tick at 25k home troops. Expansion into plains is no faster above ~6.6k troops (~26% of 25k). A ~30–40% send probably expands at full speed while keeping regen high. The coach's "60–100% first send" should be tested against replays before keeping it.
- **Bots.** The `scoring.ts:267` sentence should say "you lose only 70% of normal troops per tile against bot tribes". Their gold only comes on the eliminating hit, and since bots earn 50/tick from 0, a bot holds ~30k per minute it has lived.

### Gaps
- I did not run any formula against a replay. Every number here is read from source; the opening-ratio point above is an inference from the formulas.
- `largeTerritoryBonus` uses `sigmoid` from `engine-lib/src/Util.ts`, which I did not open. I assume a standard logistic with midpoint 300k tiles and steepness 2.5 in log space, as the comments at [EngineConfig.ts:86-100](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/7ee96860c717b1ea36870cdfa6d4bb1e9bccd6de/packages/engine/src/configuration/EngineConfig.ts#L86-L100) describe.

## 2. What do Cities, Ports, Factories, Defense Posts, SAMs, Silos and Warships do now?

### Takeaway
All structures except Defense Posts are upgradable, and a level counts as one more unit on the cost ladder. Ports and Factories are the only income buildings. Trade ships pay both port owners a distance-based amount. Trains pay both the train owner and each City/Port station owner they stop at, more for allies. SAMs are deterministic, multi-missile and slow to build. Warships now have healing and veterancy.

## 3. Terrain, defense posts and how they combine

### Takeaway
Terrain sets a per-tile base loss (`mag`) and base speed cost (`tileCost`). Defense Posts and fallout multiply both. All modifiers stack multiplicatively.

### Cited Findings
- **Base values.** Plains mag 80 / tileCost 16.5; Highland 100 / 20; Mountain 120 / 25 — [EngineConfig.ts:102-118](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/7ee96860c717b1ea36870cdfa6d4bb1e9bccd6de/packages/engine/src/configuration/EngineConfig.ts#L102-L118). Mountain costs 1.5× plains losses and is ~1.5× slower.
- **Defense Post** in range: mag ×5, tileCost ×3 — [EngineConfig.ts:527-530](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/7ee96860c717b1ea36870cdfa6d4bb1e9bccd6de/packages/engine/src/configuration/EngineConfig.ts#L527-L530). Mountain + Defense Post = 600 mag versus 80 on bare plains (7.5×).
- **Fallout.** Tiles with fallout multiply mag and tileCost by `5 − 2 × (share of land with fallout)` (2.5–5×) — [EngineConfig.ts:135-139](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/7ee96860c717b1ea36870cdfa6d4bb1e9bccd6de/packages/engine/src/configuration/EngineConfig.ts#L135-L139), [EngineConfig.ts:531-535](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/7ee96860c717b1ea36870cdfa6d4bb1e9bccd6de/packages/engine/src/configuration/EngineConfig.ts#L531-L535). ## 4. Alliances, betrayal, embargo, and nation AI at Medium

### Takeaway
Alliances last 5 minutes and are renewed by mutual agreement. Breaking one makes you a traitor and hurts your relations with every nearby player. Attacking a non-bot player triggers a 5-minute trade embargo. Medium nations ally readily in the first 3 minutes and betray only much weaker allies. They are absent from public Team lobbies.

## 5. Team-mode rules

### Takeaway
Teammates are always "friendly": they cannot attack each other and can donate troops and gold every 10 s. Each SAM shoots every enemy nuke in its range, whoever it is aimed at. Trains between teammates pay 25k to both sides. The team wins at >80% of non-fallout land combined, with no overtime in public Team games.

- **Coaching gaps for Team play.** The coach has no team-specific advice. The engine supports at least these:

## 6. Nukes

### Takeaway
Nukes destroy every unit in the outer radius, turn the blasted land into unowned fallout, and kill troops roughly in proportion to (share of land lost)^5. Launching one breaks alliances with any significantly hit player (making you a traitor). SAMs can only engage a nuke within 150 tiles of its launch point or its target.

## 7. Mechanics that matter for coaching but the coach does not model (reference sheet)

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
